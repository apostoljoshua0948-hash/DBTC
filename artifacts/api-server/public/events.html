School Events

Activities, celebrations, and programs organized by the Student Council for A.Y. 2024–2025.

Coming Up

Upcoming Events

Mark your calendars — these are the major upcoming activities of the student council this school year.

July 15, 2025

Don Bosco Foundation Day

A grand celebration honoring the founding of Don Bosco Training Center. Featuring cultural presentations, student performances, Mass, and an evening program.

Time: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Venue: School Grounds & Auditorium
Open to: All Students & Faculty
July 22–26, 2025

Intramurals 2025

The annual inter-class sports competition featuring basketball, volleyball, badminton, chess, and track and field events. Go Bosconians!

Time: 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily
Venue: School Gym & Grounds
Open to: All Enrolled Students
August 8, 2025

Science & Technology Fair

Students showcase innovative science and technology projects. Open for judging, public viewing, and participation. Best projects receive recognition and awards.

Time: 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM
Venue: School Gymnasium
October 5, 2025

Buwan ng Wika Cultural Show

A celebration of Filipino language, culture, and arts. Featuring spoken word, folk dance, oration, and a parade of traditional costumes from different Philippine regions.

Time: 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM
Venue: School Auditorium
November 22, 2025

Acquaintance Party

The annual gathering where students from all year levels get to know one another. Features games, intermission numbers, and a light fellowship dinner.

Time: 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM
Venue: School Grounds
March 2026

Graduation Rites

The culminating ceremony for graduating Grade 12 students. The Student Council assists in the preparation and coordination of the commencement exercises.

Venue: To be announced
