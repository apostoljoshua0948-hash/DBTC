About Us

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

The Student Officers of Don Bosco Training Center, Mati City uphold the Salesian mission of education, faith, and service. We work to enhance student life and represent the voice of our fellow students.

  • 🙏

    Faith & Integrity

    Rooted in the values of Saint John Bosco — serving with honesty and moral courage.

    Service & Leadership

    Putting students first in every decision, program, and initiative we undertake.

  • 🌟

    Excellence & Growth

    Striving for academic and personal excellence while lifting others along the way.
